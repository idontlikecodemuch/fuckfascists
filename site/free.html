FCK is free until Election Day.

The app

FCK shows the political contributions on file for the companies behind what you buy. Tap a business, scan a barcode, or pick the platforms you use. Public FEC records, every number linked to the filing. It shows every side and doesn’t tell you what to think. I built it on my own.

Why it’s free

More people pay attention to money in politics in the two weeks before an election than at any other time. I think people have more power than they use, and the price shouldn’t be the reason someone doesn’t look. So it’s free through November 3. On November 4 it’s $1.99 again.

The facts

  • Free on the App Store through Tuesday, November 3, 2026. $1.99 from November 4.
