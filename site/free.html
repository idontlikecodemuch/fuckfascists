Why FCK is free until Election Day

TL;DR

  • FCK is free on the App Store through Election Day, November 3. It goes back to $1.99 on November 4.
  • The app shows the political contributions on file for the companies behind what you buy, from public FEC records. It doesn’t take a side.
  • I think people have more power than they use. The records should be easy to see while people are paying attention.

I made FCK free until Election Day.

It’s an app I built on my own. You tap a business, scan a barcode, or pick the platforms you use, and it shows you the political contributions on file for the company behind it. The numbers come from the FEC. Every one links back to the filing.

It doesn’t tell you what to think. It doesn’t endorse anyone. It shows what was given, on every side, as filed, and leaves the rest to you. The only thing it keeps track of is what you chose to avoid, and that stays on your phone.

I believe people have more power than they use. Every purchase is a decision, and most of us make dozens a day without the information. For the next two weeks more people are paying attention to money in politics than at any other time. I didn’t want the price to be the reason someone didn’t look.

So it’s free through November 3. On November 4 it goes back to $1.99.

No accounts, no tracking, no servers. The code and the data are open. If something’s wrong, tell me: info@fckfascists.com.

— Christopher Shannon
