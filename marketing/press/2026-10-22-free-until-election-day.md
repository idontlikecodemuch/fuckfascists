## FCK, Financial Contribution Kit, is free until Election Day

**The campaign-finance app drops its price to zero through November 3, so cost is not the reason anyone shops without the facts.**

App Hold Media LLC today made FCK, Financial Contribution Kit, free on the App Store through Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The app, normally $1.99, lets anyone tap a business on a map, scan a product's barcode, or track the platforms they use, and see the political contributions on file for the company behind it. Every figure comes from public Federal Election Commission records and links back to the filing.

"Money moves through politics every day, and almost all of it sits in public records that almost nobody reads," said Christopher Shannon, who built the app. "For the next two weeks the price should not be the reason someone doesn't look."

**Where FCK stands.** FCK does not endorse candidates or parties. It shows contributions on every side, exactly as filed, with a link to the FEC record and a confidence label on every match. It never tells a user what to think or what to buy. The only thing it keeps score of is what a person chooses to avoid, and that score never leaves their phone.

**Why the price is zero.** The idea behind FCK is that people already hold real power and use it every day without noticing. Every purchase is a decision. The app makes the information behind that decision easy to see, and makes the decision itself worth sharing: a weekly scorecard, dropped at a random moment between Friday and Saturday, that users can post with a single tap. Election Day is when attention on money in politics peaks. Making the app free until then puts the records in more hands when more people are looking.

**Built to be trusted.** FCK has no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking and no servers of its own. All data stays on the device. The source code and the company, people and product lists are public and open source under the AGPL, so anyone can check the work.

FCK, Financial Contribution Kit, is free on the App Store for iPhone through November 3, 2026, and returns to $1.99 on November 4. More at fckfascists.com.

### About FCK

FCK, Financial Contribution Kit, is an open-source app that shows the political contributions on file for the businesses and products people use, drawn from public FEC records. It is published by App Hold Media LLC and built by one person. Website: fckfascists.com. Source: github.com/idontlikecodemuch/fuckfascists.
