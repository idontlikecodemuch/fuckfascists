---

Notifications, fixed.

• If you skipped the notifications step at setup, or reinstalled, you never got the drop or the weekly nudges, and the app wasn't even listed in your iPhone's Settings. Fixed. The app asks again after your first avoid, and on a few drop weekends, until you say no thanks.

• Info now shows whether notifications are on: a bell at the top, and a Notifications line above The Data. Tap either to turn them on, or to open the app's notification settings.

• Tapping a notification while the app is open now always lands on the right tab.

• Next week's drop notification is set even if you only open the app on drop weekends.

• Tidier Track and Map under the top banners, and the Past scorecards link is easier to tap.

No accounts. No tracking. Your activity stays on your device.
