# Hashtag + keyword plan v2: @fckfascists.app (IG first, Bluesky second, App Store)

Research date: 2026-10-02; research only, nothing posted. This replaces v1's
(`hashtag-plan.md`) *conclusions*; its platform facts stand and aren't repeated (5-tag cap,
tags classify rather than reach, Meta SIEP and the product carve-out, Bluesky feed
triggers). Numbers that couldn't be checked say "unverified".

## Principle

Every tag must **mean** something specific to this product and **amplify** (put us in an
active room that cares). FCK is a **game** that shows **disclosed campaign money** (FEC
filings, R: and D:), so each post carries one money-in-politics signal, one game signal and
the house tag. Pointed is fine; picking a side or accusing anyone isn't, so the tag can be
sharp but the caption must be accurate. Broad tags (#PixelArt, #brands, #scanner, #iOSApp,
#VideoGames) are out: no room that cares, and pixel-art rooms are hostile to AI-made sprites.

**Changed from v1:** #DarkMoney goes from "never" to rule-bound rotate. #PixelArt, #8bit and
#iOSApp are dropped. #MoneyInPolitics becomes core; #IndieGames is the one game tag; #IFCKd
is new for user scorecards. "Boycott" stays out of hashtags but in the App Store keywords.

**Boost rule.** No #DarkMoney, #MoneyInPolitics or #CampaignFinance on a boost, and no

([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/profanity/));

## #DarkMoney: what we can say, what we can't

**The fact.** Dark money is political spending "where the source of the money is not
disclosed" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_money)). Super PACs must disclose
their donors; 501(c) groups don't. FCK shows corporate PAC money that *is* disclosed and
reported to the FEC. So nothing in the app is dark money, and we must never say it is.

**The defensible frame: disclosed isn't the same as seen.** The money is on file at FEC.gov
and invisible at the register; FCK puts it on the shelf. The #DarkMoney room cares who funds
politics, and our post is the counterpoint: the money you *can* see, both columns. The news
helps: 2026 "gray money" reporting calls it a "bipartisan … arms race fueled by anonymous donors"
([The Nation, 2026-09-09](https://www.thenation.com/article/politics/gray-money-2026-midterms-super-pacs/)),
and *NRSC v. FEC* (2026-06-30) struck the federal limits on party coordinated spending
([FEC](https://www.fec.gov/updates/supreme-court-finds-limits-on-coordinated-party-expenditures-unconstitutional-in-nrsc-v-fec-609-us-____2026/)).
A month out from Nov 3, the room is active.

**Every caption under #DarkMoney carries one contrast line**, in the Sh*tposter outside voice:
- "Not dark money. Filed money. On record at FEC.gov, nowhere on the receipt."
- "Dark money gets the documentary. This is the money in plain sight."
- "Disclosed isn't the same as seen."
- "All public. Nobody shows it to you at checkout."

**Never, under this tag or anywhere:** "dark money" as a label for anything the app shows
("Amazon's dark money", "track dark money") · "secret", "hidden", "undisclosed", "exposed" or
"uncovered" about FEC data · any suggestion the totals are all of a company's political
spending (we can't see 501(c) money, by definition) · a party framing ("Republican dark
money"); both R: and D: always show · #DarkMoney on any post whose subject is a named or
recognisable person (scorecard, Track), which reads as an accusation · "dark money" in the
visible App Store text (the hidden keyword field only; see ASO).

If a post can't carry a contrast line, the nearest honest tag is #CampaignFinance.

## Where amplification actually comes from

Tags **can** classify a post, keep it visible on small exact pages (#MoneyInPolitics,
#CampaignFinance), collect user cards (#IFCKd) and trigger Bluesky feeds. They **can't**
create reach on IG (Mosseri, per v1 sources and
[Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-hashtags-reach/)), and they won't
put us on the Top tab of #IndieGames or #DarkMoney without engagement. What has to carry the load:
1. **Words the platform reads:** captions, on-screen text and VO ("campaign finance",
   "FEC.gov", "dark money", "indie game"); IG posts are Google-indexed (v1).
2. **Reels topics:** "Add a Topic", up to 3 ([Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/reels-topic)),
   matched to the topics users pick in "Your Algorithm", rolled out Dec 2025–Jan 2026
   ([SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-updates/)). Pick gaming topics; a
   politics topic invites the political-content filter.
3. **Trial Reels:** non-followers only, which matters at near-zero followers; no
   collaborators allowed ([Publer](https://blog.publer.com/instagram-trial-reels-guide/)).
   Test the #DarkMoney hook here first. Unverified whether our account has it.
4. **Collabs:** indie-game curators are realistic (e.g. @roganplaysgames, 89.1K, weekly
   indie roundups). OpenSecrets and Issue One almost certainly won't co-post with
   "FCK FASCISTS"; pitch them the data, not a collab.
5. **The scorecard loop:** each user who posts a card tagging @fckfascists.app + #IFCKd
   reaches their own followers; repost every one to Stories.
6. **Games for Change Awards:** "Best in Community Empowerment" (formerly Civics). The 2026
   window was Jan 1, 2025–Mar 31, 2026, fee $75
   ([G4C](https://festival.gamesforchange.org/2026-awards-categories)). The 2027 window is
   unverified; if it rolls forward, FCK qualifies. Reached via the festival, not a hashtag.

## App Store keywords (ship with 1.2.0)

Apple ranks on title, subtitle, keywords and **primary** category (Reference) and says: no
repeats of name/subtitle/category words, no plurals, skip generic terms like "game", commas
without spaces ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)). Words combine across
fields ([AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/app-store-keyword-research-aso)).
Keywords only change with a new version (promo text can change any time but isn't a keyword
field, [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)). Room checks: iTunes
Search API, 2026-10-02; it ignores the hidden field, so it shows who owns a query, not our rank.

**Proposed (97/100 chars):**
`dark money,boycott,campaign,finance,PAC,FEC,corporate,donations,republican,democrat,politics,game`

| Word (current → new) | Call | Why |
|---|---|---|
| political donations | → `donations` | "political" is already in the subtitle; Apple combines them. Query owned by Goods Unite Us and Wallet Vote: our room. Saves 10 chars |
| boycott | keep | Hidden field, so not copy and the voice rule doesn't bite. Query = Boycat, No Thanks, Goods Unite Us, Boycott Detective: shoppers checking brands, which matches Avoid. Never in visible text |
| PAC, FEC | keep | 3 chars each. "corporate pac" query returns "Congress Watch - PAC Money"; "fec" returns FEC Mobile |
| dark money | keep | The creator's call. Results today are money-making/gambling apps, so a relevant app may have room (unverified). Fine in a hidden field; never in the description |
| Republican, Democrat | keep both | R:/D: is the product; combos like "republican donations". Single words return partisan news apps. Symmetric, so no side taken |
| scorecard | drop | Golf and sports scoring apps |
| activism | drop | 5 Calls, TurnUp: an action room, not ours |
| civic | drop | Honda, credit unions |
| donor | drop | Blood and plasma donors; "donations" covers it |
| + campaign, finance | add | "campaign finance" = FEC Campaign Tracker, CampaignTrack50, Goods Unite Us: the exact room |
| + corporate | add | corporate donations / PAC / money |
| + politics | add | "money in politics" = Pocket Politics (a game), Integrity Index, Goods Unite Us. Unverified whether Apple stems "political" to "politics" |
| + game | add (a test) | Apple calls "game" too broad. It's here only to pair with "political"/"politics" for "political game" (Suzerain, Democracy Simulator, 270). The secondary Games category isn't indexed |

The title is camel-cased "FinancialContributionKit". One ASO guide says Apple splits it
into words, and the iTunes API matched "financial contribution" (rank 18). Unverified for
App Store search itself, so spaced title words weren't counted on.

  unless the post discloses AI-assisted art; the creator's call. Skip #uspol
