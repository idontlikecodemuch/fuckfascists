# App Store — What's New, 1.2.0 (build 10)

Clark voice, corporate neutral (voice framework §App Store listing): no FCK, no
emoji, no party claims, nothing promised that isn't in the build. Source facts:

Fresh files. Sharper scans. Random drops.

• Your scorecard shows full surnames — Van de Put, not Put.

• Track now includes Cursor, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.

• Fixed stale lookup results and two CEO sprites that never made it onto the card.

No accounts. No tracking. Your activity stays on your device.
