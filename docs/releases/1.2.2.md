Notifications, fixed.

• If you skipped the notifications step at setup, or reinstalled, you never got the drop or the weekly nudges, and the app wasn't even listed in your iPhone's Settings. Fixed. The app asks again after your first avoid, and on a few drop weekends, until you say no thanks.

• Election week: a reminder Sunday evening and Tuesday morning, with a link to find your polling place and hours on VOTE411, the League of Women Voters' nonpartisan voter guide.

• Tapping a notification while the app is open now always lands on the right tab.

• Next week's drop notification is set even if you only open the app on drop weekends.

• Tidier Track and Map under the top banners, and the Past scorecards link is easier to tap.

No accounts. No tracking. Your activity stays on your device.

## Internal release record

### Alerts (notification permission)

- **Found 2026-10-25.** The owner's phone never got a drop alert or nudge. FCK was missing from Settings → Notifications: the app had never asked iOS. The only request was onboarding's Permissions screen; in beta mode its Allow marked notifications confirmed without asking, Skip/Done never asks, and nothing asked later. Every scheduling error was swallowed. Code and web research found no other cause (no iOS 26 or Expo bug). Same behavior in 1.2.0 and 1.2.1.
- **Who else:** anyone who tapped Done or Skip without allowing notifications (no FCK entry in Settings, no way to fix it), anyone who tapped Don't Allow or later turned alerts off (never told), probably anyone who deleted and reinstalled (the onboarding flag survives in the keychain, the permission does not; to confirm), testers in beta mode. Android 13+ has the same one-time permission.
- **Fix (owner decisions 2026-10-25):**
  - Never-asked users get the iOS prompt 2.5 s after an avoid (`ALERTS_PROMPT_AFTER_AVOID_DELAY_MS`).
  - While alerts are off, a "NOTIFICATIONS OFF" banner shows on the first open inside a drop's 48h window, after that drop's reveal; every other drop, at most three times (option C: `ALERTS_ASK_EVERY_N_DROPS`, `ALERTS_ASK_MAX_SHOWS`). × hides it for that drop, "No thanks" stops it for good. Tap: the iOS prompt while it can show, else the app's Settings page.
  - Info screen (owner 2026-10-26): a pixel bell + "On"/"Off" readout at the top right of the header (design option A, HUD bell: ringing green bell / slashed grey bell; a status button rather than a switch, since a tap asks iOS or opens Settings instead of flipping) and a game-menu line "Notifications ····· On >" above THE DATA (option E) so the word is visible too. Both show the same system permission and do the same thing. History: build 15 had a full-width "Drop alerts" row at the bottom (too big and buried); build 16 a small "NOTIFICATIONS" pixel switch (caps, awkward, no state word). Six options were designed by an Opus design agent (mockups in the session scratchpad); On is `successGreen`, Off `textSecondary` (not red: off is a valid choice), labels `textPrimary` IBM Plex; blue Bungee is kept for section headings and links. Wording reviewed with the marketing lead (banner title "NOTIFICATIONS OFF", "NO THANKS" stops for good, × is not-now).
  - Onboarding's notification Allow asks iOS for real in beta mode.
  - The drop, Thursday and early-week schedulers run only while alerts are on, and again as soon as they are turned on (permission re-read on every activation, `AlertsProvider`).
  - Beta mode: long-press BUG for the permission, the queued alert identifiers and the next drop alert time.
- **Drop alert scheduling:** the alert now always targets the next drop that has not happened (`getNextScorecardDropAfter`). Before, an app opened only inside a drop's 48h window never scheduled next week's alert. The capture runner's in-window cancel and `isDropNotificationStale` are removed (they would delete next week's alert; every run now replaces the identifier).
- **Not changed:** a nudge that arrives while the app is open still isn't shown; the app is already open, so it has done its job, and Thursday has its in-app banner.

### Election Day reminders (owner 2026-10-26)

- Local notifications for people with notifications on: Sunday Nov 1, 6pm local ("Election Day is Tuesday. Make a plan…") and Tuesday Nov 3, 8am local ("It's Election Day. Polls are open today…"). 8am is before the app's 9am quiet-hours end on purpose (polls open 6–7am).
- In-app banner for everyone who opens the app, Sunday through Election Day ("ELECTION DAY IS TUESDAY" / "IT'S ELECTION DAY"); it wins over the other top banners; × or a tap hides it for that day.
- Every tap opens VOTE411 (vote411.org, League of Women Voters Education Fund, nonpartisan: polling place and hours by address, ballot info). vote.gov was considered; it is registration-focused and sends people to state sites for polling places.
- The Mon/Tue early-week nudge is skipped in election week.
- Copy by the marketing lead (Clark, nonpartisan, names VOTE411 before the tap). Dates come from `ELECTION_DAYS` (2026-11-03, 2028-11-07); the copy names no date.
- Review (Opus, 2026-10-26), fixed in build 19: reminders are queued only within 30 days of an election (a launch after Nov 3 had queued the 2028 ones); a reminder tap counts as the notification route, so a pending card reveal waits until the user is back from the browser instead of being marked seen while Safari opens; a reminder tap also hides the banner for the day; the date-dependent `pipeline.test.ts` case now uses a relative date (full Jest 729/729). Checked sound: DST (US clocks change Sun Nov 1 at 2am; 6pm and 8am local are right in New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, Anchorage), single VOTE411 open on cold start, banner priority, privacy.
- Reach: only people who have opened 1.2.2 before the reminder time get the notification (local notifications are queued by the app); everyone who opens the app Nov 1–3 sees the banner. Request expedited review citing Election Day.

### Hardening review fixes (Opus review of build 14, 2026-10-25)

- Warm notification taps route through a response listener. expo's `useLastNotificationResponse` ignored a response whose identifier matched the previous one, so with the app kept alive between weeks a tap on the next `scorecard-drop` or `early-week-nudge-1` did nothing.
- Screens under a top banner (Thursday, app update, alerts) get a zero top inset (`SafeAreaInsetsContext` override in `AppShell`); Track and Map added the status bar inset a second time.
- Past scorecards link: 44pt minimum tap target.
- The alerts toggle and banner open the app's Notifications page on iOS (`app-settings:notifications`, `openNotificationSettingsURLString`); the plain settings URL landed on Settings → Apps.
- Open items for the owner are in `docs/PROGRESS.md` (Oct 25 session): bundled donation data turns stale on 2026-12-11, `products.json` future-date pinning, capture before images load.

### Review of the 1.2.2 branch (same agent, fixed)

- A banner show now counts only after 2 s on screen with the app active, or a tap; before, a banner replaced at once (pending App Store update banner, card reveal, late reveal) used up one of the three asks.
- No after-avoid prompt when un-marking a day, or while a card reveal is on screen.
- The BETA badge no longer takes taps over the banners (readout moved to a BUG long-press).
- An empty next week no longer cancels a just-passed drop's alert within an hour of its time (Android alarms run late; quiet-hours drops wait for 9am).
- Banner height comes from layout only (an estimate could leave content under the taller alerts banner).
- If the permission can't be read, the schedulers still run.
- The Info control is a button with an On/Off value (it never toggles in place); banner VoiceOver label no longer says "Double tap".
- Known: on Android 13+ the after-avoid prompt doesn't fire (expo reports never-asked as denied); banner and toggle still ask. `PermissionsScreen.tsx` is 295 lines, over the limit before this branch.
