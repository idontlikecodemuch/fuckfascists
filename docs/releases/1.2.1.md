## Internal release record

### Scorecard

- Figures without a CEO sprite show a red pixel X (`assets/pixel/scorecard/no_sprite_x.png`, 14×14 art at 8×) in the sprite slot on the shared card and the live preview, instead of an empty slot. Owner-chosen from eight options.

### Sprites

- 49 new CEO sprites (`assets/pixel/sprites/`, 192 to 241). Owner-reviewed one by one; Ricard and Rogers took extra rounds.
- How they were made: Gemini can't take a real name or photo, so the text description carries the likeness. Opus agents compared each description with the person's photo and the previous render, rewrote it, then reviewed the first renders and applied the lessons across all 49 (`tools/img-gen/USAGE.md`). Gemini from text beat OpenAI `gpt-image-1.5` given the photo in a three-person test.
- Pipeline per sprite: compose, `remove_magenta.py --defringe`, normalize on hires (86% body height, 5% bottom margin), optimize, then manifest and `core/sprites/spriteAssets.ts`.
- Not matched by current data, bundled for later: John Ternus (Apple held at Tim Cook), John Ghingo (Hormel lists Jeff Ettinger), Jeff Schomburger (no live entity). A remote data update can switch to them without a release.
- Review files (local): `tools/img-gen/output/sprite-review-2026-10-03/`.

### Early-week notification

- New weekly local notification on Monday or Tuesday, 10am–6pm local, never in quiet hours. Eight owner-written lines; each week's line is picked first (never the same two weeks running), then a minute inside that line's own window: Monday and Tuesday lines on their day, the coffee line 9–11am, the lunch line 11am–2pm, "Today's wide open" before 2pm.
- Same local time on every device, derived from the week; nothing stored, no network.
- Sent even when the user has already logged an avoid that week (owner decision). The next 3 weeks stay queued (`EARLY_WEEK_NUDGE_WEEKS_AHEAD`), so a lapsed user gets three, then none.
- A tap opens the tab the line is about: the map for places and spending, Track for logging. Notification tap routing now lives in `app/navigation/notificationRoute.ts`.
- Own Android channel and IDs (`early-week-nudge-1..3`); never touches the Thursday nudge or the scorecard drop.

### Scan

- Close-range barcode focus (expo-camera 16.0.18, Podfile `post_install` patches, research in `docs/CAMERA_CLOSE_FOCUS_RESEARCH_2026-10-02.md`): back camera prefers the virtual triple / dual-wide device so iOS switches to the ultra-wide for close subjects; zoom starts at the wide-lens switch-over (preview looks like 1x); zoom is re-applied after every session-preset change (the `.photo` preset reset it to the ultra-wide). Every patch raises during `pod install` if expo-camera's source changes. Owner confirmed close focus on his iPhone with TestFlight build 12 (2026-10-14, relayed by the marketing session). A non-Pro check is still worth doing.
- Barcode attribution fixes for build 13 (branch `fix/barcode-attribution`; phase 0 of `docs/HOUSEHOLD_BARCODES_PLAN_2026-10-14.md`; details in `docs/BRAND_OWNERSHIP_2026-10-01.md`):
  - Walmart store brands no longer scan as food companies: `681131` (Equate, Parent's Choice) moved from Maple Leaf Foods and `078742` (Great Value, Member's Mark) from B&G Foods to Walmart.
  - A UPC-A scan is no longer matched through its `0` + UPC-A form (Barbasol had scanned as Campbell, O'Keeffe's as PepsiCo). Real EAN-13 matching is unchanged.
  - 14 six-digit prefixes in shared GS1 US blocks (81, 85, 86) that held other companies' products were removed (for example `850005` Asahi, `850028` Ottogi, `860003` PepsiCo). Touchland (`850028448`, Church & Dwight) and First Aid Beauty (`81016080`, P&G) were added at their longer prefixes.
  - Kenvue products (Tylenol, Listerine, Neutrogena, Aveeno: ten prefixes) no longer credit Johnson & Johnson; they return no bundled match until a Kenvue entity exists (1.2.2).
  - 62 verified household prefixes from the October 1 research now ship (88 brand rows, including Windex, Clorox, OxiClean, Zicam, Vicks/NyQuil, Tampax, Scott, Glad, Hefty, Durex, Enfamil and Seventh Generation). Lysol already matched through Reckitt's existing `019200`.
  - Hand corrections now live in `scripts/data/curated-prefixes.json`, merged by `scripts/sync-products-from-off.py` on every build, so a rebuild no longer undoes them.
  - The plan's 20-barcode end-to-end check: 6 right owner, 4 wrong, 10 no match before; 11 right, 1 wrong (an older Suave code in Unilever's mixed `079400` block), 8 no match after.
- Scanner product data updates from Git, like the company list (`PRODUCT_LIST_UPDATE_URL` = `assets/data/products.json` on `main`). Scans start on the bundled copy; a remote copy is accepted only if it parses, is complete (non-empty, at least 90% of current producers and exact rows) and its `_meta.generatedAt` is at least as fresh. The accepted copy is saved on the device (`runtime-data/products.json`) and used on later launches unless the bundled copy is fresher. Failures fall back silently; launch and scanning never wait on it. From this build on, a `products.json` pushed to `main` reaches installed apps on next launch, so it must come from `sync-products-from-off.py`. Info copy (`data-currency`, `data-transmitted`, `internet`) now mentions the product list.
