# FCK 1.2.0 (Build 10)

Release version for the October 2026 update. Repository metadata is aligned to
`1.2.0`, iOS build `10`, and Android version code `10`. Branch: `release/1.2.0`,
based on the shipped `v1.1.0` source.

## App Store — What's New (draft; the marketing session will finalize in app voice)

Fresh files. Sharper scans.

• FEC data refreshed September 30, 2026, including the latest 2026-cycle filings.

• The scanner now reads the short barcodes on small bottles and cans (like a 20 oz soda).

• New household coverage: SC Johnson (Windex, Ziploc, Glade, Pledge), 3M (Scotch-Brite, Post-it, Command), and Reynolds (Reynolds Wrap, Hefty), plus more cleaning brands from Clorox, Church & Dwight, P&G, and others.

• Updated CEO names for more than 180 companies.

• Full surnames on your scorecard: names like Van de Put now show in full.

• Fixed CEO sprites that weren't showing for a couple of companies.

No accounts. No tracking. Your activity stays on your device.

## Internal release record

- UPC-E decoding: `upc_e` enabled in the scanner; GS1 UPC-E to UPC-A expansion before lookup (`features/Map/barcode/normalizeBarcode.ts`). Beta feedback: Sprite 20 oz bottle.
- Household batch: `sc-johnson` (C00342246), `3m` (C00084475), `reynolds-consumer-products` (no PAC), 31 brand aliases; `clorox` linked to C00062224; `church-dwight` and `newell-brands` confirmed no PAC.
- `products.json` unchanged from 1.1.0 (see `docs/FEC_BULK_REFRESH_2026-09-30.md`, "Products").

### Data

- FEC bulk refresh 2026-09-30: 15 changed archives (2022, 2026, 2024 masters). 757 entities, 242 with committees; 1,097 people, all hydrated. Details in `docs/FEC_BULK_REFRESH_2026-09-30.md`.
- CEO names: 182 updated from the 2026-09-27 check (`tools/review/ceo-freshness-2026-09-27.json`). Apple held at Tim Cook (John Ternus CEO since 2026-09-01).

### Scorecard and sprites

- `core/utils/surname.ts`: display surnames keep particles (Van de Put), drop Jr/II/III, keep "Mars Family", join co-leads; used by scorecard rows, card donor links, and the extension popup.
- `core/sprites/spriteId.ts`: sprite ids drop punctuation and accents (J.K. Symancyk and Shane O'Kelly sprites now resolve).
- 50 new character descriptions in `tools/img-gen/characters.json`; images not generated (Gemini now declines named real people; see PROGRESS).
